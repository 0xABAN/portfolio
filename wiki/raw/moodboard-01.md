## What it is

Dark poster layout: huge white wordmark (`OUTER`), monochrome photo in a **red rectangle**, red technical numerals, thin rules, HUD glyphs, noise, checkered red strip, dense filler labels.

## Steal

- Black field + white display type + red hairlines
- Photo as inset panel with red stroke
- Side column of small mono meta + icons
- High contrast, grain, industrial ornaments

## Leave

- Pure poster (static) composition — we need scrollable site structure
- Placeholder lorem clusters as real copy

## What it is

Full-screen terminal/BIOS chrome. Top menu tabs (`SUMMARY` `FIXED` `BOOT` …). Left: dense mono dump. Right: large **ASCII/pixel portrait**. Bottom brand wordmark. Original ink is **cyan on black**.

## Steal

- Dual-pane split (log/text | ASCII art)
- Tab row as nav metaphor
- Border box framing the whole viewport
- ASCII as first-class hero media
- Boot/version status strings in the header

## Leave / remap

- **Cyan → white + red** per [color](../design/color.md)
- Aggressive repeated shock copy — tone is ours, not the ref's
